How to play

Tap a piece of land on the shelf to pick it up, then tap its place on the island, or drag it there. Each piece fits exactly one spot: match its shape to the outlined slot. Pieces with a round arrow can be turned with a tap. When the last piece clicks in, the island comes alive.

Frequently asked questions

How do I turn off sound, music or vibration?

Tap the gear in the top right corner and switch them off. On Android, vibration also follows your phone's settings.

What is the "Ask a grown-up" question?

It is a parental gate. Links out of the app (this page and the Privacy Policy) and sharing sit behind a simple multiplication question, so young children don't leave the game by accident.

Can I play a finished island again?

Yes. On the sky map, a finished island has a big hand button to visit it and play with it, and a small blue button to solve its puzzle again.

Does the game need the internet?

No. It works fully offline. It has no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases.

I deleted the app. Can I get my progress back?

Progress is saved only on your device and is not uploaded anywhere, so it is removed together with the app.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 15 or later, and Android phones with Android 7.1 or later.
