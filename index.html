About the game

A storm has scattered the cat's floating islands across the sky. Pick up chunky pieces of land, fit each one into its place on the puzzle board, and watch the island come alive: animals hop out, waterfalls pour off the edge and the cat jumps on to celebrate.

Finished islands stay on your sky map. Visit them any time to play: tap an animal to make it leap, tap the land to make it sing.

  • No ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases
  • No data collected: progress stays on your device
  • Plays offline, no timers or pressure
