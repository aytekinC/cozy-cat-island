Privacy Policy

Cozy Cat Island · Last updated: October 25, 2026

Türkçe metin aşağıdadır.

In short

  • The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.
  • The game works offline. Your progress is saved only on your device.

Who we are

Cozy Cat Island (the "app") is made by Aytekin Coşkun, an independent developer ("we", "us"). You can reach us at y41161714@gmail.com.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any other personal information, and it does not use advertising identifiers. It does not send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

What is stored on your device

To remember where you are in the game, the app saves a small file on your device with the islands you have finished, your stars and best times, and your settings (sound, music and vibration). This file never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Sharing

After finishing an island, a grown-up can choose to share a short text (the island number, the time and the stars). This button is behind a parental gate. It opens the standard iOS share sheet; you decide where the text goes, and the app does not see or store what you do there. The app or service you share to has its own privacy policy.

Links out of the app

The "For Parents" area, behind a parental gate, links to this Privacy Policy and to our help page. They open in Safari. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages; when you visit it, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Our website uses no cookies and no analytics.

Third-party services

The app is built with the Unity game engine. Unity's usage statistics, diagnostics, analytics, ads and other online services are turned off in this app, so no data is sent to Unity. The app contains no other third-party code that collects data.

Children

The app is made to be safe for players of all ages, including children. Because we collect no personal information at all, we do not collect personal information from children under 13 (as defined by the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) or from anyone else. Links out of the app and sharing are kept behind a parental gate.

Data retention and deletion

We do not hold any data about you, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete. Your game progress lives only on your device and is removed when you delete the app. If you email us, we use your email only to answer you, and we delete our correspondence on request.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its use. Since the app collects none, there is no personal data to request; you are welcome to contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles data (for example by adding a feature that needs data), we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released, and change the date above.
